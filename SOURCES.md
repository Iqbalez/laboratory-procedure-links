# Authoritative sources

Source journal: STAR Protocols. Repository access: Europe PMC, https://europepmc.org/ . Documented API: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/ . Individual article XML endpoint: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/{PMCID}/fullTextXML . Example of an included CC BY4.0 article: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10192636/ .

The source index query was JOURNAL:"STAR Protoc" AND OPEN_ACCESS:Y AND LICENSE:"cc by", resultType=core, pageSize=1000. It returned1,155 hits; the first1,000 metadata records were captured on2026-09-13. Every included article is separately checked for the exact CC BY4.0 permission URL in its JATS license element. Index-level licensing is not treated as definitive. The frozen source_report.json records all exclusions, including unavailable and differently licensed documents.
